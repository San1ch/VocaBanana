# Privacy Policy for VocaBanana

**Effective date:** August 19, 2026

VocaBanana ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles your data.

### 1. Information We Collect
VocaBanana does not collect, store, or process any personal user data on external servers. 

### 2. Google Drive Integration
The app uses Google Drive API with restricted access (`appDataFolder` scope) exclusively to:
- Save and restore your personal app backups (`vocabanana_backup.zip`).

**Important:** The application can only access files created by itself inside the hidden application data folder. It cannot access, read, or modify any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

### 3. Data Sharing
We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your data with any third parties. All backup data remains strictly between your device and your personal Google Drive storage.

### 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via GitHub repository issues.
